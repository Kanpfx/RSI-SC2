# ThorDrop

**Core idea:**
Reach Thor and Medivac production quickly, move individual Thors by Medivac to create durable drop pressure, and cycle damaged pairs through a safe repair point while Marines secure the ground game.

**General guidance:**

- Check emergency_defence first, then thor_drop_pressure. Otherwise use opening, thor_tech, first_drop, thor_drop_pressure.
- Pair one Medivac with one Thor and morph every Thor to High Impact Mode when it is created.
- If a Medivac is below roughly one quarter health or a Thor below roughly two fifths health, send the pair to a safe repair point instead of forcing the drop.
- Continue Marine, SCV, Supply Depot, and expansion production behind healthy Thor-Medivac cycles.

## opening

**When to choose:**

- The Factory, Armory, and Starport technology chain is incomplete.

**Phase goal:**

- Reach Thor and Medivac production with basic home defence.

**Guidance:**

- Open Supply Depot, Barracks, two Refineries, Reaper, Orbital Command, Factory, early Marines, second Supply Depot, Armory, Factory Tech Lab, and Starport.
- Do not spend early resources on an expansion before the first Thor and Medivac infrastructure is active.

## thor_tech

**When to choose:**

- The Armory, Factory Tech Lab, or Starport is under construction or ready.
- No Thor-Medivac pair is ready to leave.

**Phase goal:**

- Produce the first healthy drop pair and establish a fallback repair point.

**Guidance:**

- Start Thor production as soon as the Factory Tech Lab and Armory allow it, and produce Medivacs from the Starport.
- Keep Marines at home until the first Thor begins pressure.
- Prepare a safe healing point between the enemy side and home.

## first_drop

**When to choose:**

- At least one Thor and one Medivac are available.
- The first Thor drop has not yet established pressure.

**Phase goal:**

- Create the first safe, high-value Thor drop.

**Guidance:**

- Load one Thor into one Medivac and fly toward the attack target while avoiding visible anti-air.
- Unload only to threaten workers, production, or isolated units, and keep the Medivac ready to reload the Thor.

## thor_drop_pressure

**When to choose:**

- One or more Thor-Medivac pairs are actively dropping, or additional pairs are being produced behind the first drop.

**Phase goal:**

- Sustain repeated pressure without losing expensive air and ground units.

**Guidance:**

- Keep each Thor paired with a Medivac, attack exposed targets, and reposition from anti-air or superior ground forces.
- Send damaged pairs to the repair point; repair Thors with SCVs before returning.
- Continue Thor and Medivac production, Marine support, and expansions.

## emergency_defence

**When to choose:**

- Enemy forces threaten workers, the main base, or the Factory, Armory, and Starport chain.

**Phase goal:**

- Protect critical technology without losing healthy drop assets unnecessarily.

**Guidance:**

- Use Marines and nearby Thors to defend first and redirect a drop home when its mobility prevents a larger loss.
- Pause aggressive loading until the base is stable.
