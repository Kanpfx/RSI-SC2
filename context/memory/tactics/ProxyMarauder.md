# ProxyMarauder

**Core idea:**
Establish two forward Barracks with Tech Labs, use early Marauders and Concussive Shells for sustained ground pressure, then transition into a compact Marine-Marauder-Medivac army if the proxy does not end the game.

**General guidance:**

- Select the phase whose criteria best match the current observed state, using timing only as a rough guide.
- Keep Marauders as the only combat unit before the bio transition; do not add unrelated units, production, technology, expansion, or static defense that delays the proxy timing.
- Use one forward area in the direction of enemy_main for both Barracks, preferably outside immediate enemy vision when a usable location is available, and preserve nearby builders when practical.
- Group Marauders before engaging, kite ground units with Concussive Shells, and retreat from unfavorable fights instead of feeding reinforcements one at a time.
- After the bio transition, do not exceed three total bases, 60 workers, six Barracks, one Factory, two Starports, or four Refineries.

## proxy_opening

**When to choose:**

- Fewer than two Barracks exist or are under construction at the selected proxy location.
- Typically within 0-2 minutes; timing is only a rough guide.

**Phase goal:**

- Start the fixed two-Barracks proxy while preserving the minimum economy needed for Marauder production.

**Guidance:**

- At 12 supply, train one SCV; at 13 supply, build one Supply Depot at the main base and train one more SCV.
- Reserve about two SCVs for one forward area in the direction of enemy_main, then use available forward workers to start exactly two Barracks there.
- Start one Refinery at the main base after the first proxy Barracks begins construction, and assign enough workers to obtain gas for Tech Labs and Marauders.
- Do not expand, add a third Barracks, or start Factory, Starport, Engineering Bay, Armory, or other unrelated technology.
- Keep early worker spending modest until both proxy Barracks have started; do not let additional SCV production delay them.
- Finish this phase once two proxy Barracks exist or are under construction.

## marauder_preparation

**When to choose:**

- Two proxy Barracks exist or are under construction.
- Fewer than two Marauders are ready, or Concussive Shells is not yet complete.

**Phase goal:**

- Complete the minimum technology and assemble the first usable Marauder group without expanding the build.

**Guidance:**

- Complete both proxy Barracks and attach one Tech Lab to each; do not build more than two Barracks in this phase.
- Research Concussive Shells from the first ready Tech Lab and begin Marauder production immediately from every available Tech Lab.
- Reserve minerals, vespene, and supply for continuous Marauder production; avoid Orbital Command, extra workers, or other spending if it delays the first two Marauders.
- Maintain a 100% Marauder production focus with the existing two proxy Barracks and do not request additional production structures in this phase.
- Keep the first Marauders together near the proxy and use nearby builders to protect construction or block small enemy units without sacrificing them needlessly.
- Finish this phase when at least two Marauders are ready and Concussive Shells is complete or nearly complete.

## marauder_pressure

**When to choose:**

- At least two Marauders are ready and the bio transition has not begun.
- This normally continues until 26 workers or approximately 6:30, whichever occurs first.

**Phase goal:**

- Exploit fast forward reinforcements with continuous Marauder pressure before the opponent can stabilize.

**Guidance:**

- Continue producing only Marauders from the two proxy Barracks and start pressure with the first grouped force rather than waiting for a large army.
- Keep reinforcements together with the main group; do not send individual Marauders into enemy units or static defense.
- Use stutter-step and Concussive Shells to kite ground units, focus armored combat units first, and disengage when the local fight is unfavorable.
- Marauders cannot attack air and are inefficient against workers and light units; when no favorable army target exists, attack exposed production, technology, expansions, or the worker line as a secondary target.
- Do not force a fortified choke. Contain the enemy, deny an exposed expansion, or move to another known weak location while preserving the Marauders.
- After Marauder production is stable, resume SCV production up to about 26 workers and take the natural only when minerals exceed roughly 500 and the current army can maintain pressure.
- Keep the army at 100% Marauders and the production scale at exactly two Barracks until the bio transition begins.

## bio_transition

**When to choose:**

- The worker count has reached approximately 26, or game time has reached approximately 6:30 and the initial pressure has not ended the game.

**Phase goal:**

- Convert the surviving proxy into a sustainable Marine-Marauder-Medivac army without uncontrolled economic or production growth.

**Guidance:**

- Develop toward approximately 50% Marines, 30% Marauders, and 20% Medivacs; keep Marauders as the armored-ground core and Marines as support against light and air units.
- Keep safe proxy Barracks for forward reinforcement, replace unsafe production at home, and add Barracks gradually only when existing production stays busy and resources accumulate.
- Add one Factory only as the prerequisite for Starports, then add up to two Starports for Medivacs; do not produce unrelated Factory or Starport combat units.
- Add Refineries, Supply Depots, workers, and expansions only as needed to sustain the stated army composition.
- Research Combat Shield and infantry weapon and armor upgrades after the second base is established; do not let upgrades interrupt army production during active pressure.
- Fight as one supported bio group, keep Medivacs behind the infantry, focus dangerous ground units, and retreat from concentrated splash damage or clearly unfavorable positions.
- Do not exceed 60 workers, three total bases, six Barracks, one Factory, two Starports, or four Refineries; stop requesting each category after its cap is reached.
