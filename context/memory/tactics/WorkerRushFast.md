# WorkerRushFast

**Core idea:**
Pull SCVs immediately at game start for the fastest possible worker attack, relying on group cohesion, focus fire, and repairs rather than any economy or infrastructure.

**General guidance:**

- Check emergency_defence first, then immediate_assault. Otherwise use immediate_assault, sustain_or_end.
- Start moving at once with up to ten SCVs; do not wait to train workers, build supply, or gather a normal economic lead.
- Keep attacking SCVs together, use a repair group for low-health workers, and do not donate isolated SCVs to enemy defenders.
- This is the most committed worker rush. It has no intended macro recovery if it fails to gain a decisive advantage.

## immediate_assault

**When to choose:**

- The game has started and the initial SCV group is crossing the map or beginning its first fight.

**Phase goal:**

- Create the earliest possible worker engagement.

**Guidance:**

- Immediately send up to ten SCVs to the enemy base and keep them compact.
- Target exposed workers and weak units first.
- If an enemy wall is closed, wait for an opening or attack targets outside it rather than feeding into the choke.
- Use healthy workers to fight and surround while damaged workers move behind for repair.

## sustain_or_end

**When to choose:**

- The initial worker clash has resolved into continued pressure, a possible base kill, or a stalled attack.

**Phase goal:**

- Finish a winning attack or preserve the remaining workers from a lost one.

**Guidance:**

- If the worker line is collapsing, keep SCVs together and finish workers, production, and the town hall by accessibility.
- If defenders can win the local fight, preserve retreating workers rather than feeding reinforcements.
- Use any later Marines as reinforcement but do not delay the worker attack waiting for them.

## emergency_defence

**When to choose:**

- A direct counterattack would end the game before the worker rush succeeds.

**Phase goal:**

- Choose the action most likely to avoid or deliver immediate defeat.

**Guidance:**

- Bring back only workers required to prevent immediate loss; otherwise keep the pressure group committed.
- If both bases are exposed, favor the action most likely to end the game immediately rather than beginning normal macro.
