# MassMine

**Core idea:**
Use fast Widow Mine and Medivac production to defend the home economy, repeatedly threaten enemy mineral lines with mine drops, and grow into a mine-heavy bio army.

**General guidance:**

- Check emergency_defence first, then mine_drop_pressure. Otherwise use opening, mine_setup, map_control, bio_transition.
- Keep two Widow Mines near the main ramp and two near each completed base mineral line before committing the remaining Mines to drops.
- Do not send a Medivac without enough healthy Mines to create a meaningful threat. Preserve damaged Medivacs by dropping cargo safely and retreating.
- Keep producing SCVs, Supply Depots, and expansions once the first Mine and Medivac production is underway.

## opening

**When to choose:**

- The initial Factory and Starport production chain is not yet established.

**Phase goal:**

- Reach Widow Mine and Medivac production without falling behind in early safety.

**Guidance:**

- Open Supply Depot, Barracks, two Refineries, Reaper, Orbital Command, Factory, Barracks Reactor, Supply Depot, Armory, second Factory, and Starport.
- Use the early Reaper to scout or deter pressure, then return it to defend if the worker line is threatened.
- Start the first pair of Widow Mines as soon as the Factory can produce them.

## mine_setup

**When to choose:**

- Widow Mine or Medivac production has started.
- The first defensive Mine positions and first four-Mine drop group are not both ready.

**Phase goal:**

- Establish a safe Mine defence and prepare the first drop.

**Guidance:**

- Prioritize Widow Mines and Medivacs until the main ramp, mineral lines, and first drop group are covered.
- Research Drilling Claws first; add High Capacity Fuel Tanks and infantry upgrades when the economy supports them.
- Burrow Mines at the main ramp and mineral lines, spread them so one enemy spell cannot remove all local defence.
- Keep the main force near the natural while the Mine count is still fragile.

## mine_drop_pressure

**When to choose:**

- At least one healthy Medivac and four available Widow Mines exist.
- The main base is not under a serious ground attack.

**Phase goal:**

- Force the opponent to defend several mineral lines while retaining a safe home defence.

**Guidance:**

- Load four Mines into a healthy Medivac and target a completed enemy town hall with workers.
- Unload Mines where workers must pass, then retreat the Medivac from anti-air instead of waiting for a losing fight.
- Use unassigned Mines to control approaches and likely counterattack routes; return surviving drop Mines to the attacking or defensive pool after a mission.

## bio_transition

**When to choose:**

- Two or more bases are established.
- The Mine and Medivac core is stable and pure Mine production is no longer sufficient.

**Phase goal:**

- Turn the early disruption into a sustainable Mine, Marine, Marauder, and Medivac army.

**Guidance:**

- Shift the main composition toward Marines and Marauders while retaining Mines and Medivacs for zoning and drops.
- Continue infantry weapons and armor upgrades, workers, expansions, and production structures as resources grow.
- Add Missile Turrets around bases when resources allow and an Engineering Bay is ready.

## emergency_defence

**When to choose:**

- Enemy ground forces threaten a town hall, main ramp, or worker line.

**Phase goal:**

- Hold the base without sacrificing the Mine defence network.

**Guidance:**

- Defend first with nearby Mines, bio units, and only necessary SCVs.
- Cancel or delay an outgoing drop if it would leave the main base exposed, then resume the matching normal phase after the attack is held.
