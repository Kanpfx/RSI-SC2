# Reapers

**Core idea:**
Use a fast four-Barracks Reaper opening to establish early mobile pressure, then add Hellion support and controlled economic growth while preserving the Reaper force and repeatedly attacking weak enemy locations.

**General guidance:**

- Use the phases in the listed order and do not return to an earlier phase because units or structures are later lost.
- Keep Reapers as the core army and Hellions as mobile support; use Marines only as a limited mineral-heavy fallback when gas temporarily prevents Reaper production.
- Preserve the mobile army and prefer exposed targets or favorable local fights over direct engagements with concentrated enemy forces or static defence.
- When several enemy locations are available, prefer the location with the weakest local ground defence; if similarly favorable, prefer the closer target.
- Count ready and pending structures together when planning construction, and stay within the intended limits of 6 Barracks, 1 Factory, 4 bases, about 60 workers, and 4 Refineries.

## opening_setup

**When to choose:**

- The initial two-Barracks, two-Refinery, first-Reaper, and Orbital Command setup is not complete.
- Typically within 0-2 minutes; timing is only a rough guide.

**Phase goal:**

- Complete the initial Reaper setup quickly and prepare for the four-Barracks pressure.

**Guidance:**

- At 12 supply, build one Supply Depot, then train two SCVs.
- At 14 supply, start the first Barracks and first Refinery.
- Still around 14 supply, train one SCV, start the second Refinery, then train one more SCV and start the second Barracks.
- Produce the first Reaper as soon as the first Barracks and gas are available, then upgrade the Command Center to an Orbital Command.
- Use the first Reaper for safe scouting and exposed targets, but preserve it rather than committing into concentrated defence.
- Keep the opening compact: no more than two Barracks and two Refineries before this phase is complete.
- Do not expand, start a Factory, research upgrades, or spend on unrelated production or technology during this phase.
- Finish this phase when two Barracks and two Refineries are established, the first Reaper is ready or in production, and the Orbital Command is ready or upgrading.

## four_barracks_pressure

**When to choose:**

- The opening setup is complete, but the four-Barracks Reaper opening and first Factory have not yet been established.

**Phase goal:**

- Build the initial Reaper force, complete four Barracks, and start the Factory while applying controlled early pressure.

**Guidance:**

- After the initial setup, start the third Barracks and produce roughly two additional Reapers.
- Train one SCV and add the next Supply Depot before becoming supply blocked, then start the fourth Barracks.
- Produce roughly three more Reapers for about six Reapers produced in total, then start one Factory; Reaper losses do not delay this progression.
- Keep available Barracks producing Reapers when resources and supply allow, but do not build more than four Barracks during this phase.
- Regroup nearby reinforcements before attacking defended positions rather than sending individual Reapers across the map.
- Scout around the enemy main and likely expansion routes; attack exposed workers, isolated units, proxies, or undefended production rather than blindly A-Moving into the enemy base.
- Avoid concentrated ground armies and static defence; use Reaper mobility and Grenades (`KD8CHARGE_KD8CHARGE`) to take favorable trades or disengage, with roughly 60% health only as a safety reference in dangerous fights.
- Do not expand, research upgrades, build an Engineering Bay, or add unrelated technology before the Factory has started.
- Finish this phase when four Barracks are established or pending and the first Factory has started.

## hellion_macro_transition

**When to choose:**

- Four Barracks are established and the Factory has started, but Hellion production and the natural expansion have not both begun.

**Phase goal:**

- Activate Hellion support and the second-base economy without interrupting Reaper pressure.

**Guidance:**

- Continue Reaper production from the four Barracks while the Factory completes.
- As soon as the Factory is ready, explicitly begin Hellion production; having the Factory or unlocking Hellions does not substitute for actually training Hellions.
- Keep Reaper and Hellion production active separately when possible; if the action interface requires one composition, use approximately 75% Reaper and 25% Hellion as a practical fallback rather than a strict long-term ratio.
- Begin the natural expansion once the Factory has started and the first Hellion can be supported without stopping core Reaper production.
- During this transition, prioritize Reaper production, first Hellion production, the natural expansion, supply and SCVs before upgrades or additional production structures.
- Continue Reaper harassment against exposed targets, but move or scout safely when no favorable target is visible instead of issuing a blind full-group attack toward the enemy main.
- Keep four Barracks, one Factory, and at most one expansion pending; do not add a fifth Barracks, Engineering Bay, third base, or optional technology yet.
- Finish this phase when at least one Hellion is ready or in production and the second Command Center is ready or under construction.

## sustained_mobile_pressure

**When to choose:**

- Hellion production has begun and the natural expansion is established or under construction.

**Phase goal:**

- Maintain repeated Reaper-Hellion pressure while scaling economy and production within controlled limits.

**Guidance:**

- Keep Reapers as the primary army output and continuously use the Factory for Hellion support when resources allow; use limited Marines only when minerals accumulate while gas temporarily prevents Reaper production.
- Maintain active Reaper-Hellion production before secondary spending, and verify that intended units are actually ready, pending, or training rather than repeatedly requesting technology.
- Grow toward roughly 22 workers per established base and stop around 60 workers; add Refineries progressively as gas demand increases, but do not exceed four Refineries.
- Expand one base at a time: establish the natural before the third base and the third before the fourth, and do not exceed four total bases.
- Keep four Barracks as the normal baseline; add a fifth and later a sixth only when existing Barracks remain actively used and minerals continue accumulating around 700 or more, and never exceed six Barracks.
- After the natural is established and at least three Refineries are active, begin Infantry Weapons Level 1 and Infantry Armor Level 1 when they will not interrupt Reaper or Hellion production.
- When several enemy bases are known, pressure the location with the weakest local ground defence; prioritize workers, exposed expansions, isolated units, and production, and rotate elsewhere when the enemy concentrates its army.
- When no favorable target is visible, regroup reinforcements, preserve damaged Reapers, and scout or reposition toward likely expansions rather than forcing a direct engagement.
- Avoid concentrated Marine-Marauder-Tank forces, heavy static defence, and unrelated Starport or late-game technology; defensive Engineering Bay or Planetary Fortress investment is optional and should only respond to a clearly exposed or repeatedly threatened outer base.
