# Turtle

**Core idea:**
Build a heavily fortified multi-base position with Marines and Siege Tanks, then transition into Battlecruisers while Planetary Fortresses, Bunkers, Turrets, and defensive Tanks make counterattacks expensive.

**General guidance:**

- Check emergency_defence first, then fortress_growth. Otherwise use opening, ground_fortification, fortress_growth, battlecruiser_transition.
- Defend rather than force early attacks. Marines and Tanks are base defenders until the fortified economy is secure.
- Build up to two Bunkers near each town hall, then add Missile Turrets once an Engineering Bay is ready and resources are available.
- Grow to three bases when safe, upgrade outer Command Centers to Planetary Fortresses, and keep SCV and Supply Depot production running behind every defensive investment.

## opening

**When to choose:**

- The first expansion, Barracks, gas, and Orbital Command setup is incomplete.

**Phase goal:**

- Establish a safe two-base economy and first ground defence.

**Guidance:**

- Build a Supply Depot and expand early, then add a Barracks and two Refineries.
- Upgrade Command Centers toward Orbital Commands as the economy permits and produce Marines while defensive infrastructure starts.
- Do not take an early all-in fight; protect the natural and focus on economy and safety.

## ground_fortification

**When to choose:**

- The opening build is complete.
- The army is primarily Marines and Siege Tanks with fewer than four gas buildings.

**Phase goal:**

- Create a ground shell that can hold early and midgame attacks.

**Guidance:**

- Produce a Marine-Tank defensive force, keep the first Tank sieged at a useful home defensive point, and position later Tanks to cover bases.
- Build up to two Bunkers near each town hall and hold pressure rather than chasing it across the map.
- Research High Capacity Fuel Tanks and building armor, and add an Engineering Bay for further defence.

## fortress_growth

**When to choose:**

- A stable defensive army exists.
- The bot is expanding toward three bases or adding four or more gas buildings.

**Phase goal:**

- Turn safe map control into a fortified multi-base economy.

**Guidance:**

- Expand until three bases are established, increase worker and gas income, and convert suitable Command Centers to Planetary Fortresses.
- Add Missile Turrets around each base when resources exceed the defensive threshold, aiming for broad anti-air coverage.
- Keep Bunkers, Tanks, and Planetary Fortresses positioned so an opponent must fight into prepared defences.

## battlecruiser_transition

**When to choose:**

- The economy has at least four gas buildings or the composition is beginning to include Battlecruisers alongside Siege Tanks.

**Phase goal:**

- Add a powerful air army without opening the fortified economy to a counterattack.

**Guidance:**

- Transition toward roughly seven Battlecruisers for every three Siege Tanks while preserving the defensive ground shell.
- Research Battlecruiser specializations when the gas economy supports them.
- Use Battlecruisers for safe map control, defence, and selective harassment; fortify new bases before deep attacks.

## emergency_defence

**When to choose:**

- Enemy forces threaten a town hall, worker line, or unfinished defensive position.

**Phase goal:**

- Hold the threatened base and restore the defensive shell.

**Guidance:**

- Defend with nearby Marines, Tanks, Bunkers, Planetary Fortresses, and Battlecruisers before spending on further greed or technology.
- Use the shortest safe reinforcement path and avoid un-sieging all Tanks if it exposes another base.
- After holding, rebuild lost defensive structures before returning to growth.
