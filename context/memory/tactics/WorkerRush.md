# WorkerRush

**Core idea:**
Send a large early SCV group across the map after a very short setup, use worker focus fire and repairs to win the first base fight, and add Marines only as a follow-up.

**General guidance:**

- Check emergency_defence first, then worker_assault. Otherwise use opening, worker_assault, marine_followup.
- Start the worker attack at the early timing with up to eleven SCVs. Keep the main group compact and avoid isolated trades.
- When an attacking SCV is below about 40 percent health, move it into the repair group; return it to combat when healthy enough or too few workers remain to sustain repairs.
- This tactic is all-in in spirit: do not delay the attack for standard macro, technology, or expansion.

## opening

**When to choose:**

- The early SCV attack group has not yet been sent.

**Phase goal:**

- Reach the worker attack timing immediately.

**Guidance:**

- Train three SCVs immediately and prepare to pull the attack group at the early timing.
- Do not wait for normal production structures before moving out.

## worker_assault

**When to choose:**

- The SCV group is moving toward the enemy or fighting at the enemy base.

**Phase goal:**

- Break the worker line through grouped focus fire and repair cycling.

**Guidance:**

- Send up to eleven SCVs as one group toward the attack target and briefly compact them with nearby minerals if needed.
- Focus exposed workers and weak early units.
- If an enemy ramp is walled, seek workers or openings instead of wasting the group on the wall.
- Keep low-health SCVs behind the healthy frontline for repair.

## marine_followup

**When to choose:**

- The worker fight is ongoing or has created an opening.
- Marines are available to reinforce it.

**Phase goal:**

- Extend worker pressure with basic ranged support.

**Guidance:**

- Send new Marines toward the same attack target.
- Use Marines to pressure workers and production while SCVs repair, tank damage, or retreat.
- Regroup small survivors rather than walking them alone into a stronger enemy army.

## emergency_defence

**When to choose:**

- The enemy counterattacks the main base and immediate loss is possible.

**Phase goal:**

- Prevent defeat while retaining as much attack pressure as possible.

**Guidance:**

- Return only enough workers and Marines to stop immediate loss.
- If the worker assault has failed, prioritize survival rather than feeding more units.
