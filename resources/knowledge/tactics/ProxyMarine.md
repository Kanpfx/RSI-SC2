# ProxyMarine

**Core idea:**
Establish three forward Barracks and use nonstop Marine production to overwhelm the opponent before converting a surviving proxy into sustained bio pressure.

**General guidance:**

- Check emergency_defence first, then proxy_pressure. Otherwise use opening, proxy_construction, proxy_pressure, marine_macro.
- Use three SCVs to build three forward Barracks and protect the proxy from small enemy worker pulls when possible.
- Keep producing Marines and rally them toward the main squad; do not delay the first attack for gas technology.
- Before a second base is established, prioritize minerals, Supply Depots, and Marine count; after it is safe, add basic infantry upgrades and normal production support.

## opening

**When to choose:**

- The three proxy workers have not yet begun forward construction.

**Phase goal:**

- Start the fastest practical three-Barracks Marine proxy.

**Guidance:**

- Train early SCVs, build a Supply Depot at 13 supply, and send three workers to a pathable forward location.
- Avoid early gas and expansion spending.

## proxy_construction

**When to choose:**

- Forward Barracks are under construction.
- Fewer than three are ready.

**Phase goal:**

- Finish the proxy and build the first grouped Marine force.

**Guidance:**

- Finish three forward Barracks and repair or fight with proxy SCVs when enemy workers try to stop construction.
- Start Marine production from each ready Barracks and rally the first Marines together.

## proxy_pressure

**When to choose:**

- At least one forward Barracks is ready.
- Marines are gathering near the proxy or attacking the enemy.

**Phase goal:**

- Use continuous Marine reinforcements to break the enemy economy or contain it.

**Guidance:**

- Attack workers, production, or exposed defensive units with grouped Marines.
- Do not trickle Marines into a fortified ramp; attack an exposed base or contain the enemy if the entrance is sealed.
- Maintain Supply Depots so forward production does not stop.

## marine_macro

**When to choose:**

- The first pressure has created space, or a second town hall has been established.

**Phase goal:**

- Extend the proxy into a mineral-heavy Marine game.

**Guidance:**

- Expand when safe, keep a Marine-focused composition, add gas after the second base, and start Combat Shield plus infantry upgrades.
- Add more Barracks when minerals accumulate while using the proxy to keep reinforcements close to the opponent.

## emergency_defence

**When to choose:**

- A hostile force threatens the main base or worker line while most Marines are forward.

**Phase goal:**

- Stabilize home without throwing away the whole forward army.

**Guidance:**

- Recall enough Marines to protect workers and production, pulling SCVs only when necessary.
- Resume pressure after the main base is stable.
