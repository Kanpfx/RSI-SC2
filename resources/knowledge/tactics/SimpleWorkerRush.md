# Simple64

**Core idea:**
Stay on one base, build Barracks, mass Marines, and attack early.

**General guidance:**

- Keep making SCVs and Marines.
- Do not expand.
- Do not build gas, Factory, Starport, upgrades, or other units.
- Spend excess minerals on more Barracks.
- Keep the army together.

## build

**When to choose:**

- Early game, while the Marine army is still small.

**Phase goal:**
Produce Marines as quickly as possible.

**Guidance:**

- Build Supply Depot, then Barracks.
- Add Barracks until there are about 3-4.
- Produce Marines continuously.
- Keep producing enough SCVs to support the Barracks.
- Keep all Marines at home until a reasonable group has formed.

## attack

**When to choose:**

- A group of Marines is available and we are no longer waiting for basic production.

**Phase goal:**
Destroy the enemy.

**Guidance:**

- Attack the enemy base with all Marines together.
- Continue producing Marines and send new Marines to join the attack.
- Attack enemy units first when they block the army, otherwise attack workers and production structures.
- Do not retreat unless the army is almost completely destroyed.
- After destroying the enemy main, attack all remembered enemy structures.
- If all remembered enemy structures have been destroyed, sweep the map and attack until the game ends.
