# ProxyReaperWithPf

**Core idea:**
Use two forward Barracks for early Reaper harassment, then establish a forward Command Center that becomes a Planetary Fortress while transitioning into Reaper-Cyclone pressure.

**General guidance:**

- Check emergency_defence first, then forward_fortress. Otherwise use opening, proxy_barracks, reaper_pressure, forward_fortress, cyclone_transition.
- Keep two workers on the proxy only until the two forward Barracks are complete; preserve the designated Command Center builder afterward.
- Use Reapers for harassment and scouting, not losing fights against static defence. Add Cyclones when the army and resources are sufficient.
- The forward Command Center is an aggressive anchor, not a reason to abandon SCVs, Supply Depots, and safe production at home.

## opening

**When to choose:**

- The first two forward Barracks are not yet under construction.

**Phase goal:**

- Establish early forward Reaper production.

**Guidance:**

- Train two early SCVs, build a Supply Depot, and send both workers to a forward proxy location.
- Keep the opening light on optional technology.

## proxy_barracks

**When to choose:**

- Forward Barracks are under construction or only one is ready.

**Phase goal:**

- Complete the Reaper production anchor and protect it.

**Guidance:**

- Complete two forward Barracks and start Reapers from every ready Barracks.
- Use the first Reapers to protect builders and harass without committing into static defence.

## reaper_pressure

**When to choose:**

- Two proxy Barracks are complete.
- The forward Command Center is not yet complete near the enemy.

**Phase goal:**

- Use mobility to buy time and space for the forward Command Center.

**Guidance:**

- Harass workers and weak production while keeping Reapers able to escape.
- Send one SCV to an enemy-side expansion location and start a Command Center when resources allow.
- Use Reapers to deny enemy workers and vulnerable units around the construction site.

## forward_fortress

**When to choose:**

- A forward Command Center is under construction or complete near the enemy side.
- It is not yet a finished Planetary Fortress.

**Phase goal:**

- Turn the forward Command Center into a durable pressure point.

**Guidance:**

- Protect the Command Center with Reapers and supporting units.
- If it must fly, move it to the intended location and land safely; upgrade it to a Planetary Fortress when resources permit.

## cyclone_transition

**When to choose:**

- The forward fortress is established, the game is past the early timing, or resources support Cyclones.

**Phase goal:**

- Sustain pressure with Reaper-Cyclone while growing the economy.

**Guidance:**

- Transition toward Cyclones as the main fighting unit and Reapers as mobile harassment.
- Expand when the army is large enough or resources are high, and add Engineering Bay upgrades after a second base.
- Keep the forward Planetary Fortress useful but retreat from unfavorable fights.

## emergency_defence

**When to choose:**

- Enemy forces directly threaten the main base, workers, or essential production.

**Phase goal:**

- Preserve the main economy over an unsalvageable forward position.

**Guidance:**

- Defend first with nearby Reapers, Cyclones, and necessary SCVs.
- If the forward Command Center cannot be saved safely, treat it as expendable and resume from the stable home economy.
