## Mechanics

- SCV: builds on-site; gathers via HARVEST_GATHER_SCV; repairs via EFFECT_REPAIR_SCV (costs resources).
- REFINERY: built on a geyser; required for gas collection.
- Supply: SUPPLYDEPOT provides +8; maximum 200; COMMANDCENTER also provides supply.
- SUPPLYDEPOT ↔ SUPPLYDEPOTLOWERED: MORPH_SUPPLYDEPOT_LOWER / MORPH_SUPPLYDEPOT_RAISE; controls ground passage.
- Add-ons: only one attached TECHLAB or REACTOR per producer; TECHLAB unlocks advanced production/research, REACTOR permits 2 basic-unit queues.
- Lift/Land: COMMANDCENTER, ORBITALCOMMAND, BARRACKS, FACTORY, STARPORT can lift/land; lift detaches add-on; landing can reconnect. Flying buildings cannot produce.
- Base morph: COMMANDCENTER → ORBITALCOMMAND / PLANETARYFORTRESS (exclusive); PLANETARYFORTRESS cannot lift.
- ORBITALCOMMAND: CALLDOWNMULE_CALLDOWNMULE (minerals), SCANNERSWEEP_SCAN (vision/detection), SUPPLYDROP_SUPPLYDROP (+8 supply at depot).
- BUNKER: LOAD_BUNKER / UNLOADALL_BUNKER; garrisoned infantry attack; bunker has no weapon itself.
- Salvage: EFFECT_SALVAGE refunds part of salvageable structures' cost.
- Detection: RAVEN, MISSILETURRET, SCANNERSWEEP_SCAN reveal cloaked/burrowed units; SENSORTOWER is radar, not detection.
- Cloak: GHOST / BANSHEE require PERSONALCLOAKING / BANSHEECLOAK; cloak uses energy.
- WIDOWMINE ↔ WIDOWMINEBURROWED: BURROWDOWN_WIDOWMINE / BURROWUP_WIDOWMINE; fires only while burrowed.
- HELLION ↔ HELLIONTANK: MORPH_HELLBAT / MORPH_HELLION (requires ARMORY).
- SIEGETANK ↔ SIEGETANKSIEGED: SIEGEMODE_SIEGEMODE / UNSIEGE_UNSIEGE.
- VIKINGFIGHTER ↔ VIKINGASSAULT: MORPH_VIKINGASSAULTMODE / MORPH_VIKINGFIGHTERMODE.
- LIBERATOR ↔ LIBERATORAG: MORPH_LIBERATORAGMODE / MORPH_LIBERATORAAMODE.
- THOR ↔ THORAP: MORPH_THORHIGHIMPACTMODE / MORPH_THOREXPLOSIVEMODE.
- NUKE: GHOSTACADEMY uses BUILD_NUKE (requires FACTORY); GHOST uses TACNUKESTRIKE_NUKECALLDOWN.
- Burning: Terran structures below 1/3 health gradually lose health until repaired.

## Unit roles

- SCV: worker; gather/build/repair.
- MARINE: ground/air ranged; EFFECT_STIM_MARINE; SHIELDWALL upgrade.
- REAPER: ground scout/harass; cliff jump, passive regen, KD8CHARGE_KD8CHARGE.
- MARAUDER: armored-ground damage; EFFECT_STIM_MARAUDER; PUNISHERGRENADES slow.
- GHOST: ground/air support; EMP_EMP, EFFECT_GHOSTSNIPE, BEHAVIOR_CLOAKON_GHOST, TACNUKESTRIKE_NUKECALLDOWN.
- HELLION: fast anti-light ground splash; transforms into HELLIONTANK.
- HELLIONTANK: durable close-range ground splash; biological/mechanical.
- WIDOWMINE: burrowed ground/air ambush; splash, long reload.
- SIEGETANK: ground artillery; long-range splash in SIEGETANKSIEGED, immobile while sieged.
- CYCLONE: mobile ground/air; LOCKON_LOCKON.
- THOR: heavy ground/air; switchable anti-air modes.
- VIKINGFIGHTER: air-to-air; VIKINGASSAULT attacks ground.
- MEDIVAC: transport/heals biological units; EFFECT_MEDIVACIGNITEAFTERBURNERS.
- LIBERATOR: air splash; LIBERATORAG attacks ground in a fixed zone.
- BANSHEE: air-to-ground; BEHAVIOR_CLOAKON_BANSHEE after BANSHEECLOAK.
- RAVEN: flying detector/support; RAVENBUILD_AUTOTURRET, EFFECT_ANTIARMORMISSILE, EFFECT_INTERFERENCEMATRIX (upgrade).
- BATTLECRUISER: heavy ground/air; EFFECT_TACTICALJUMP; YAMATO_YAMATOGUN after BATTLECRUISERENABLESPECIALIZATIONS.
