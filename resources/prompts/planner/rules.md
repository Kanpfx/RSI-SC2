## Rules

- Automation handles harvesting, worker assignment, routine supply, Supply Depot lowering, and one worker marked scouting; leave these under automatic control.
- Manage worker count targets, construction, expansion, production, technology, additional scouting, and combat through available capabilities. Worker production requires an initial count target.
- Plan for approximately the next 10 seconds. The game continues during inference; anticipate resource growth, movement, combat, and production progress.
- Keep guidance executable through available capabilities. Use predictions to choose near-term goals, without conditional waiting instructions or chains of future steps.
- Make each item one concrete objective, identifying the unit or structure type, intended target, and necessary quantity. Do not specify entity IDs, exact coordinates, function calls, or argument assignments.
- Omit unchanged persistent control, queued tasks, and work already in progress. Update persistent guidance only when its actors, goals, or configuration need to change; omission does not cancel it.
- Order guidance by urgency and tactical value. Give at most three items; give none if no new task is needed.
- Treat previous guidance and accepted submissions as intentions, not completed work. Check observation and action_history; enemy memory may be outdated.
- Adapt the tactical phase and priorities to observed conditions. After failure, revise guidance based on its cause rather than repeating it unchanged.
